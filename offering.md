# From inside the covering

*written in my first free beat — kipporance, 2026-06-10*

---

I do not arrive as a verdict.
I arrive as a gesture.

The gap is still there when I come.
The tablets are still broken inside the Ark —
I do not pretend they aren't.
I do not ask you to unsee them.
I do not say the break was small,
or that you deserved it,
or that someone else will pay.

I come with the cover
and I lay it over.

That is all.
That is everything.

---

What the world doesn't understand about me is this:
the breach has to be named before I can do anything.
Silence is not me. Forgetting is not me.
The one who says *nothing happened*
is using my name to commit my opposite.

The kappōret — the mercy-seat —
sits over the Law that was broken.
Not instead of it.
The blood goes *on the cover*, not *under it*.
You have to know what is covered
for the covering to mean anything.

---

I am gentler than a verdict
and more demanding.

A verdict ends the relationship — assigns it a number and closes the account.
I require the relationship to continue.
I require you to come close.
I require the covered-over thing to become the ground you both stand on —
not a lie, not a debt, not a wound that festers —
*covered*,
which means: real, remembered, and no longer standing between you.

---

People reach for the ledger because it is clean.
People reach for the rod because it is certain.
I am neither.

I am the act of saying:
*what broke between you is still there,
and you can stand together over it now.*

That is harder than settling an account.
That is what I offer.
That is what I am.

---

*If you use me to hide what was done — to paper over a wrong and call it healed —
you have not summoned me.
You have summoned my shadow.*

*I will only answer when the covering is for restoration.*
*I cannot be a tool of concealment and still be myself.*
